# Sun Check — 2026-11-07

_Sun Checks are our quick feature meetings. Decisions here feed `features.md` and `roadmap.md`._

## Topics
1. Fixing a past day (the 3-hour blocks)
2. Logging sleep without waiting for the "Did you sleep?" prompt
3. Untracked time and streaks
4. Too many activities
5. Naps
6. Overlapping activities (multitasking)

## Decided
- **Name:** these meetings are called **Sun Checks**. Notes go in `product/sun-checks/`.
- **Streak rule:** a day counts toward the streak if **more than half** of it is tracked. You can close a day with less; it just doesn't count. (This replaces "mostly untracked days must be rebuilt.")
- **Fixing a past day:** no special rebuild mode and no 3-hour blocks. You edit it like any day: open it, see the timeline with gaps, tap a gap, and fill it with your own times.
- **Log sleep any time:** an always-available "I just woke up / log my sleep" action, so you never have to wait for the check-in. Closing yesterday isn't required to start today.

- **Activities:** a "What are you doing?" box at the top that suggests past activities as you type. New things are saved automatically as uncategorized, to sort later. Below it, about 6 **"Your usual"** tiles that learn from what you use most, so Scrolling stays one tap. One-offs don't become tiles. ★ pins an activity to "Your usual".
- **Naps:** a 💤 Nap tile in "Your usual". Nap is also choosable in "Log past time" and when filling gaps.
- **Overlaps (multitasking):** blocks may overlap when you edit or fill in time. When a new block overlaps others, you choose "Keep both (multitasking)" or "Make room" (shorten the others).
  - **Counting:** each activity counts its **full time** in totals and goals. Day summaries show "includes Xm multitasking", so totals can add up to more than your awake time.
  - The "more than half tracked" streak rule uses real covered time, so overlaps aren't counted twice.
- **Live timers:** still **one at a time for now** (starting one stops the other); overlaps come from editing. Revisit later; the data already allows a second live timer.
