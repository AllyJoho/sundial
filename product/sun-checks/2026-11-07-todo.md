# Sun Check — 2026-11-07 (3): To-do list on Today

## Topics
1. The diary's Morning / Afternoon / Evening sections
2. A to-do list at the bottom of Today (before the full Plan tab exists)
3. What details a task can have
4. Remembering tasks that can only be done at certain hours (e.g., phone calls to companies, only 9–5)
5. Unfinished tasks at the end of the day

## Decided
- **Diary:** no parts-of-day sections. It's one continuous timeline with a single "+ Add". (Reverses part of Sun Check (2).)
- **"To do today"** at the bottom of Today, above "Complete today". Type a line to add something at any time; tap ✓ when done. Done items show for the rest of that day, then drop off (kept as history).
- **"Later" list** collapsed under it, sorted by due date. "Do today" moves an item up. When the Plan tab is built, Later moves there.
- **Task details, all super optional** (folded under "More details"):
  - **Notes / instructions**, several lines, so tomorrow-you knows exactly what to do.
  - **Due date.** Shows "due Fri". After it passes: a plain "was due Tue" with Do today / Remove. Never red.
  - **Only-during hours**: days + times, e.g. Mon–Fri 9–5. Shows "open now · until 5 PM" or "opens 9 AM".
  - Steps checklist: **later**, not now.
- **Remembering:**
  - An **"Open now" card** at the top of Today while a task's hours are open (for today *and* Later tasks).
  - **"Add to iPhone Calendar"** (an .ics file, so Calendar sends the alert) is a **setting, off by default**. Lots of people won't want it, and the owner doesn't want it while testing.
- **Unfinished at goodnight:** each unchecked task gets **Tomorrow / Later / Remove**.
  - Default: Tomorrow if it's the only one left; **Later** if 2+ are left, so today's list never piles up (research: missed plans shouldn't become a guilt list).
  - "Later" keeps everything (notes, due date, hours). "Remove" is for one-day things.

## Status
Built on 2026-11-07. Tested in a phone-sized browser; not yet on a real iPhone.

## Research follow-ups (for the research session)
- Remembering to do something *during a window* (business hours) without push notifications: what works best? (prospective memory; time-based vs event-based cues)
- Dreaded small tasks (phone calls, emails): what actually gets them done? (task aversiveness, phone anxiety, "two-minute" starts)
- Do written notes/instructions on a task ("next action") improve follow-through the next day?
- How many items on a daily to-do list before it backfires for someone who feels behind?
