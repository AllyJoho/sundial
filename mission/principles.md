# Principles

Every feature must pass these. If a feature conflicts with one, the principle wins.

1. **Reflect reality, don't judge it.** We're a mirror, not a referee. Honest data matters more than "good" data.
2. **Kind, not shaming.** Use neutral language: "untracked," never "wasted." No guilt-trip notifications. Bad days are still logged days.
3. **Showing up is the win.** Streaks reward logging, not performing well. A day of 10 hours of scrolling still counts if you logged it.
4. **Consistency over perfection.** For sleep especially, a steady schedule at "weird" hours is better than a chaotic "normal" one.
5. **Meet people where they are.** Goals start from someone's *actual* baseline and move in small steps.
6. **Your day is yours.** A day starts when you wake up, not at midnight. Nocturnal people, shift workers, and all-nighters all work.
7. **Logging must be effortless.** Every extra tap makes people less likely to use it. If you can do it in one tap, make it one tap.
8. **Everything is editable.** Forgot to clock out? Forgot goodnight? Fix it easily, especially yesterday.
9. **Recovery is always possible.** A missed day shouldn't erase progress, but getting it back takes a little real effort (reconstruct the day).
10. **Your data is yours.** You can always export it.
