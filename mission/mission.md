# Mission

## In one sentence
Help people whose lives have fallen apart see where their time actually goes, gently and honestly, and slowly build days they feel good about.

## Who it's for
- First: me. A student whose life is a mess right now.
- Then: anyone in a rough patch. That includes chaotic sleep, all-nighters, nocturnal schedules, random naps, and days that disappear into scrolling.
- Not built for people who already have it together and want to squeeze out more productivity. Those people can use it too, but they aren't who we design for.

## Why it exists
- Calorie trackers work because **writing it down makes you see it**. Nobody looks at where their hours go in the same way.
- Most productivity tools assume a normal 9-to-5, sleep-at-night life. When your life isn't like that, they either break or make you feel like a failure.
- Screen time apps only measure your phone, and they mostly scold. Sundial measures your **whole day**, including sleep, and it **reflects reality** instead of judging it.

## What success looks like
- I open it every day, even on bad days.
- After a month, I can see real patterns in my sleep and time that I didn't know about.
- My sleep gets more consistent, and my time moves toward my goals, even if it's slow.
- It feels like a friend keeping track with me, not a parent grading me.

## Why I'm building it (personal)
- To get my own life back in order.
- To learn how to vibecode and build a real app.
- Maybe someday to turn it into a real product.
